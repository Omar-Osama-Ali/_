Cookies Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This site does not set any cookies of its own. There's no analytics tool, no advertising pixel, and no first-party cookie used to remember you between visits.

Essential technical storage

Your browser may still keep small amounts of local data purely to make the site function — for example, remembering that you opened the mobile navigation menu during your visit. This stays in your browser, is not sent anywhere, and isn't used to identify or track you.

Links to other services

If you follow a link to WhatsApp, LinkedIn, Instapay or WeChat, that service may set its own cookies once you're on their site. Those are covered by their own cookies policies, not this one.

If this changes

If analytics or a similar tool is added to this site in future, this page will be updated first to say what's used and why, and, where required, to ask for your consent before it runs.
