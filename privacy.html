Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This page explains what happens to your information when you visit this website, omarosama's portfolio site (referred to here as "this site").

What this site does not collect

This site does not have a contact form, does not run analytics or tracking scripts, and does not use advertising cookies. There is no server-side account system, and no visitor data is stored by this site.

What happens when you contact me

The Contact section links directly to external services: email, WhatsApp, LinkedIn, Instapay and WeChat. When you use one of these links, you leave this site and the information you share is handled under that service's own privacy policy, not this one. I'd encourage you to check the relevant provider's policy before sending sensitive information.

Hosting and technical logs

Like most websites, the hosting provider used for this site may keep basic technical logs (such as IP address and request time) for security and reliability purposes. These logs are a function of standard web hosting rather than something added to this site, and this site does not access or process them for any other purpose.

Third-party embeds

This site does not embed third-party widgets, fonts, or scripts from external domains. Any link to a third-party destination is a plain link that opens that service directly, as disclosed above.

Your choices

Because no personal data is collected on this site itself, there is nothing here to request, correct or delete. If you've contacted me through one of the linked services and want a message or file deleted from my side, email me and I'll remove it.

Changes to this policy

If how this site works changes — for example, if a contact form or analytics tool is added later — this page will be updated to describe it before it goes live, along with the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to omar.osama2011@yahoo.com.
